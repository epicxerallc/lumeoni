Our story

Born between
concrete & canopy.

Lumeoni Studio grew from a simple wish: give children learning materials that feel calm enough to live with, beautiful enough to keep, and rooted in the natural world beyond the screen.

The beginning
“It started the way most small things do — with our own children.”

Living in Dubai, surrounded by glass and concrete, made the natural world feel even more important. We wanted pages that could introduce a child to an acorn, a feather, a frog or a changing leaf without shouting for attention.

So we made the first packs ourselves. The rule became simple: nothing ships unless the art would be worth framing.

What guides the studio

Quiet design. Real curiosity.

01

Gentle before busy

We leave breathing room on the page and choose calm palettes that support focus rather than compete with it.

02

Nature before novelty

Our themes come from things children can notice outside: seasons, tracks, roots, wings, water, seeds and weather.

03

Useful before clever

A beautiful resource still needs to print well, cut cleanly and make sense on a real family table.

04

Human before perfect

We like the softness of watercolour, the irregular edge of paper and the warmth of something that feels made, not manufactured.

Who we make for

Homes, classrooms & curious corners.

Lumeoni is for Montessori and Waldorf educators, homeschooling families, parents looking for screen-light activities, and anyone creating a nursery or learning corner that feels more like a small nature table than a worksheet station.
