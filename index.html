Why Lumeoni exists

A little more nature, even in the city.

“The rule from day one: nothing ships unless the art would be worth framing.”

It began with our own children in Dubai — surrounded by glass and concrete, yet endlessly curious about the natural world. When the learning materials we found felt too loud or too generic, we started making our own.
