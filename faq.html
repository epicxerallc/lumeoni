Regular printer paper is fine for worksheets. For flashcards and 3-part cards, 160–250 gsm cardstock or watercolour-style paper gives a sturdier, more tactile result.
The studio workflow is designed to support both A4 and US Letter print-ready versions. Confirm the exact files included in the product detail before ordering.
