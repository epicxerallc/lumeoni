A note for grown-ups

Screen-light means knowing when to stop.

The Kids’ Room is intentionally tiny. It is designed as a quiet bridge back to hands-on play — a short pause, then a printable, a walk outside, a magnifying glass, or a tray of leaves.

Watercolour nature printables and gentle learning resources for curious children, ages 2–12.
